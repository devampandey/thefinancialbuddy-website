## It's rarely a willpower problem

When a budget falls apart in the first few weeks, the usual explanation is "I just don't have discipline." In practice, the more common cause is a budget that was unrealistic from the start.

## The most common mistakes

**Setting spending targets too low.** If your "dining out" budget assumes you'll never eat out, one dinner with friends feels like failure instead of a normal, planned expense.

**Forgetting irregular expenses.** Car registration, annual subscriptions, and holiday spending don't show up every month — until they do, and blow up an otherwise fine budget.

**Copying someone else's percentages exactly.** The 50/30/20 rule is a useful starting benchmark, not a mandate. High cost-of-living areas often need a different split.

## A more durable approach

Track real spending for one full month before setting any targets. Build in a buffer category for irregular expenses. Revisit the budget monthly for the first quarter — it should get easier to hit, not harder, as it's tuned to reality.
