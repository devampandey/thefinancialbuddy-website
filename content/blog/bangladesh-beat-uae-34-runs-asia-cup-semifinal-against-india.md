Bangladesh Women booked their place in the Women's Asia Cup semifinals on Tuesday night, beating hosts United Arab Emirates by 34 runs in a decisive Group B fixture at the Dubai International Cricket Stadium to set up a last-four clash with India.

## How the Match Unfolded

Put in to bat after UAE captain Esha Oza won the toss and chose to field first, Bangladesh posted 103 for 7 in their 20 overs. Sharmin Akhter top-scored with a brisk 38 off 31 balls, while skipper Nigar Sultana contributed 23, as UAE bowler Suraksha Kotte claimed three wickets to keep the total in check.

Chasing 104 for victory, UAE never quite found the momentum they needed and were bowled out for 69 in their full 20 overs, falling 34 runs short. Bangladesh's bowling attack shared the wickets around, with left-arm spinner Rabeya Khan the pick of the bowlers with three scalps, while Marufa Akter and Nahida Akter picked up two apiece to seal the win.

## Securing the Fourth Semifinal Spot

The result confirmed Bangladesh as the fourth and final semifinalist of the tournament, joining group toppers India, defending champions Sri Lanka, and Pakistan in the knockout stage. UAE's defeat ends what had been a spirited campaign for the tournament debutants, who had earlier claimed a notable win over Indonesia to keep their semifinal hopes alive heading into Tuesday's match.

For Bangladesh, the win caps a mixed group stage in which they beat Indonesia comfortably but lost a close contest to Sri Lanka, who had already sealed their own semifinal berth. Tuesday's victory over UAE proved decisive in the battle for the second qualification spot from Group B, with net run rate having been in play as a possible tie-breaker before Bangladesh settled the matter emphatically on the field.

## India Awaits in the Last Four

With the group phase now complete, attention turns to the knockout stage, where India, who finished top of Group A after winning all three of their matches, will face Bangladesh in the first semifinal on September 10 at 8 pm IST in Dubai. The second semifinal, featuring Sri Lanka and Pakistan, is scheduled for September 11, also at 8 pm IST.

## Why the Result Matters

For India, the semifinal draw against Bangladesh presents a contest against a side that showed both promise and inconsistency through the group stage, having thrashed one opponent convincingly while narrowly losing to Sri Lanka's defending champions. Bangladesh, for their part, will head into the knockout match aiming to carry forward the momentum of Tuesday's comprehensive win as they look to spring an upset against a India side that has been unbeaten through the tournament so far. With the tournament now down to its final four teams, Wednesday and Thursday's semifinals are set to decide who advances to contest the Women's Asia Cup title.
