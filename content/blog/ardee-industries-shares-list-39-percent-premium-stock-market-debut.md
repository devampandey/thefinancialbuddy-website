Shares of Ardee Industries made a strong debut on the stock exchanges on Wednesday, listing well above their issue price and rewarding investors who had piled into one of the most heavily subscribed initial public offerings of the month. The recycling and recovery solutions company's stock opened at Rs 72 on the National Stock Exchange, a gain of nearly 36 percent over its Rs 53 issue price, while on the BSE the shares debuted even higher at Rs 73.60, up close to 39 percent.

## A blockbuster subscription number

Ardee Industries' Rs 426-crore initial public offering was open for bidding between August 5 and August 7, and the response from investors was overwhelming. The issue was subscribed 133.66 times overall, drawing bids for more than 780 crore shares against the roughly 5.8 crore shares on offer, translating into close to 41.2 lakh applications. That level of demand had already pushed the stock's grey market premium up sharply in the days leading up to listing, setting the stage for Wednesday's strong opening trade.

The scale of oversubscription placed Ardee Industries among the more richly bid-for mainboard offerings of the year, reflecting investor appetite for smaller, niche industrial plays even as broader market sentiment stayed choppy on the back of elevated crude oil prices and geopolitical uncertainty.

## What Ardee Industries does

Ardee Industries operates in the recycling and recovery solutions space, a segment that has drawn growing investor interest as Indian companies scale up capacity to process industrial by-products and material recovery amid tightening environmental regulation. The IPO proceeds are expected to support the company's expansion plans and working capital needs, though the exact break-up of fund deployment will become clearer as the company begins reporting as a listed entity.

## Listing day context

Ardee Industries was one of several stocks in the spotlight on Wednesday, a day that also saw a large number of companies, including Tata Motors, Grasim Industries and Apollo Hospitals, scheduled to report their June-quarter results. The benchmark Sensex and Nifty themselves had a choppy start to the session, making Ardee Industries' clean listing pop stand out against a more mixed backdrop for the broader market.

Market participants said the strong debut reflects continued investor confidence in the primary market even as secondary market indices wrestle with external headwinds such as rising crude prices and uncertainty over a resolution in the Strait of Hormuz standoff. With Ardee Industries now trading as a listed company, attention will shift to how the stock holds its listing-day gains in subsequent sessions and whether the promoters and pre-IPO investors show any signs of profit booking once the mandatory lock-in periods for various investor categories begin to lift.
