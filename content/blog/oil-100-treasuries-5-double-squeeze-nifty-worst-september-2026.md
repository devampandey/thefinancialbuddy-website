September 2026 was the month two shocks hit India at once: front-month Brent crude jumped 14% to above $100 as the Strait of Hormuz conflict dragged on, and the US Federal Reserve's first rate hike since 2023 pushed 10-year Treasury yields past 5%. That combination drove foreign investors out of Indian equities, with Rs 44,013 crore of net FII selling, pushed the rupee past 96 per dollar, and sent the Nifty 50 down 6.1% and the Sensex 5.8%, the Nifty's worst September since 2018.
