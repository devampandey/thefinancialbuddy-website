Indian equity benchmarks closed higher on Wednesday, but the day's real story sat one level below the headline numbers: a sharp rally in metal stocks driven by copper prices touching an all-time high, even as weakness in IT shares kept the broader advance in check.

## The Numbers

The BSE Sensex rose 299.17 points, or 0.40 percent, to close at 74,828.25, while the NSE Nifty 50 added 117.80 points, or 0.50 percent, to settle at 23,446.80. The gains came after a choppier session on Tuesday, with Wednesday's advance helped by firmer Asian markets and crude oil holding below the $100-a-barrel mark, even as Middle East tensions kept a floor under energy prices.

## Copper's Record Run Lifts Metal Stocks

The Nifty Metal index closed up about 2.18 percent, comfortably the best-performing sector on the day. Hindalco Industries gained roughly 3.17 percent and Tata Steel climbed about 3.16 percent, while JSW Steel added around 2.43 percent, placing all three among the Nifty 50's strongest performers. Jindal Steel and SAIL also featured among the day's gainers.

The trigger was copper, not steel. US copper futures touched a fresh all-time high of $6.92 per pound, having risen close to 4.6 percent over the preceding five sessions, as persistent demand and ongoing production and supply-chain disruptions tightened the market. Rising copper prices tend to lift sentiment across the broader metals complex, even for steelmakers whose direct exposure to copper is limited, because they are read as a signal of resilient global industrial demand.

## IT Stocks Remain the Drag

Information technology shares continued to underperform, with the sector index shedding around 1 percent even as the benchmarks advanced. Infosys, TCS and Wipro were among the Nifty 50's laggards, extending a stretch of weakness that has weighed on the sector for weeks amid investor concern that faster adoption of AI tools could dent demand for traditional IT services work. Bajaj Finance and Apollo Hospitals were among the day's other top gainers outside the metals space, rounding out a session where financials and healthcare names also found buyers.

## What's Ahead

Markets will have a fresh listing to watch on Thursday, with NSE's shares set to debut on the BSE after its Rs 22,561.57-crore initial public offering was subscribed 5.71 times, one of the more closely tracked primary market events of the year. Traders will also be watching whether copper's rally has further room to run, since a sustained move higher in industrial metal prices could keep providing support to India's metal pack even if global cues elsewhere turn choppy.
