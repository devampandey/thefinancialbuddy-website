Realme expanded its budget smartphone lineup in India on Wednesday with the launch of the P4s 5G, a gaming-focused device aimed at price-conscious buyers who still want flagship-adjacent performance features.

## Key specifications

The P4s 5G is built around a MediaTek Dimensity 7400 Ultra processor, paired with up to 8GB of RAM that can be virtually expanded, and storage options that go up to 256GB. The display is a 6.8-inch AMOLED panel with 1.5K resolution, sourced from Samsung, and supports a 144Hz refresh rate along with HDR10+ certification, features more commonly associated with pricier devices.

Battery life is anchored by an 8,000mAh cell with 80W fast charging, which Realme claims can deliver up to three days of typical usage on a single charge. On the imaging side, the phone carries a 50-megapixel primary rear camera with optical image stabilisation alongside an 8-megapixel secondary sensor, and a 16-megapixel front camera for selfies and video calls. The device also carries IP68, IP69 and IP66 ratings, giving it dust and water resistance well above what is typical in its price segment.

## Pricing and availability

The P4s 5G is being offered in three memory configurations, with prices across the lineup ranging from roughly Rs 37,999 to Rs 41,999. Realme is also offering a Rs 3,000 instant bank discount alongside a 12-month no-cost EMI option to sweeten the deal for buyers. The phone will be sold through Flipkart and Realme's own online store, with the first open sale scheduled for September 3. It comes in two colour finishes, Titanium Grey and Electric Cyan.

## Why it matters

The launch lands at a time when Indian smartphone buyers have been squeezed by repeated price increases from larger players. Samsung, OnePlus and OPPO have all raised prices on parts of their lineups in recent weeks as global component costs and currency pressures bite, making Realme's aggressive positioning on battery capacity, display quality and gaming performance in the P4s 5G a pointed play for value-conscious buyers who might otherwise trade down or delay an upgrade.

The device also arrives amid a broader wave of activity in India's budget 5G segment, with rivals such as Vivo preparing to launch its own T5 5G model in the same price band in the coming days, and Philips separately readying its own 5G smartphone for the Indian market. With gaming-oriented features such as high refresh-rate displays and large batteries increasingly becoming standard even in sub-Rs 40,000 phones, the P4s 5G reflects how competitive India's mid-tier smartphone segment has become, with manufacturers racing to out-spec each other on hardware while keeping prices within reach of a price-sensitive but performance-hungry buyer base.
