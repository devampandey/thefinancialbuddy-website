India's build-up to next week's Test series in Sri Lanka took an early hit on Saturday when Yashasvi Jaiswal was dismissed for a duck on the second day of the three-day warm-up fixture against a Sri Lanka Cricket XI in Colombo, before KL Rahul and Devdutt Padikkal steadied the innings through the first session.

## Early Setback, Then Recovery

India came into their reply after Sri Lanka Cricket XI had piled up 363 for 8 by the close of the first day at the Nondescripts Cricket Club ground. Jaiswal, one of the first names on the Test team sheet, was removed without troubling the scorers, handing the tourists an early setback as they began chasing down the hosts' total. Rahul and Padikkal responded by building a watchful partnership through to the lunch interval, taking India to 67 for 1 at the break.

The pair's approach reflected the broader purpose of the fixture: less about the scoreboard and more about India's batters getting used to the turning, low-bounce surfaces they are likely to encounter once the two-match Test series against Sri Lanka begins in Galle on August 15. Warm-up games ahead of subcontinent tours are typically used by touring sides to fine-tune technique against spin rather than chase quick runs, and Saturday's cautious start fit that pattern.

## Gill's Fitness in Focus

Complicating India's build-up is the fitness of skipper Shubman Gill, who sat out the entire first day after injuring the ring finger on his right hand during a practice session ahead of the match. The BCCI's medical staff have been monitoring the injury, and with India batting on day two, Gill's availability to walk out at some point in the innings remained an open question through the morning session. His participation, or lack of it, is likely to be a talking point through the rest of the match as the team management weighs the risk of aggravating the injury against the value of match practice before the series opener.

## Day One Set the Tone

Sri Lanka Cricket XI's total of 363 for 8 was built around contributions from openers Nishan Madushka and Ravindu Rasantha, who put on a brisk stand before India's bowlers, led by the spin trio of Ravindra Jadeja, Kuldeep Yadav and Manav Suthar, hauled the innings back with two wickets apiece. That effort meant India went into their own innings with a stiff target to chase in a match that carries no result significance beyond preparation, but every session of batting and bowling against unfamiliar conditions matters with the first Test just a week away.

For India, the tour match serves as the final proper hit-out before the series proper, with the second Test to follow in Colombo from August 23. How the top order — and Gill's finger — hold up over the remaining two days in this warm-up fixture will shape team combinations heading into Galle.
