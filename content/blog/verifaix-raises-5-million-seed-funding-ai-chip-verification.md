VerifAIX, a startup building an AI-native platform to verify the correctness of complex semiconductor designs, has raised $5 million in seed funding, the company confirmed as part of a wider day of Indian startup funding announcements on Wednesday. The round was co-led by Endiya Partners and Bluehill VC.

## What the Company Does

Founded in 2024 by Madhulima Tewari, Kenneth Roe and Avner Landver, VerifAIX is tackling a problem that has become more urgent as chip designs grow more complex and AI tools increasingly assist in generating parts of that design: verifying that a chip actually does what its specification says it should before it is manufactured. The company describes its platform as a "verification trust layer" that connects design specifications, register-transfer level (RTL) code and verification assets, using a combination of AI reasoning and formal verification methods.

Central to VerifAIX's pitch is what it calls its "Formal Brain," a system meant to automate the abstraction and decomposition of large, complex chip designs into pieces that can be verified more rigorously and quickly than traditional manual verification workflows allow. The company says its approach is aimed at semiconductor and IP companies, processor and AI-accelerator developers, hyperscale cloud providers and systems companies -- customers for whom a verification error caught late, or missed entirely, can be extremely costly.

## Use of the Funding

VerifAIX said the fresh capital will go toward product development, deepening customer deployments and expanding its engineering teams across three geographies: the United States, India and Israel. The multi-country engineering structure reflects an increasingly common pattern among deep-tech and semiconductor startups, which often draw on Israeli and Indian engineering talent pools alongside US-based go-to-market teams.

## Why It Matters for India's Chip Ambitions

The funding round lands at a moment when India is pushing hard to build out its domestic semiconductor ecosystem, from fabrication incentives to design-services growth, with the SEMICON India event this year underscoring the government's continued focus on the sector. Verification and design-validation tools like VerifAIX's are a less visible but critical part of that supply chain: as more chip design work, including AI-assisted design, moves to India, robust verification infrastructure becomes essential to ensuring locally designed silicon is production-ready.

VerifAIX was one of several Indian companies to close funding rounds on Wednesday, alongside smaller raises in sectors ranging from aesthetic-surgery technology to fashion discovery, as investors including Bluehill VC, Zeropearl VC and AJVC continued to back early-stage bets across India's technology landscape.
