Kolkata-based Lumino Industries opened its initial public offering for subscription on Thursday, becoming the latest mainboard issue to test investor appetite in what has been an active year for Indian IPOs. The offer will remain open until August 31, with the company targeting a listing on the BSE and NSE in the following days.

## Issue Structure and Pricing

The IPO aims to raise around Rs 700 crore, split between a fresh issue of Rs 500 crore and an offer for sale of up to Rs 200 crore by existing shareholders. Shares are priced in a band of Rs 78 to Rs 82 each, with a minimum lot size of 182 shares, meaning retail investors need to commit roughly Rs 14,924 at the upper end of the band to make a single-lot application. The allocation structure reserves 50% of the issue for qualified institutional buyers, 15% for non-institutional investors, and 35% for retail investors, a fairly standard split for an issue of this size.

## What the Company Does

Lumino Industries operates in the cable manufacturing and engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) space, sectors that have benefited from India's continued push on power transmission, renewable energy evacuation infrastructure, and grid modernization. Brokerage commentary ahead of the listing has pointed to the company's integrated business model, spanning manufacturing and project execution, as well as what several analysts described as a comparatively strong return on net worth relative to listed peers in the cable and EPC space.

## Valuation and Market Sentiment

Multiple brokerages have flagged that the issue is priced at a reasonable discount to comparable listed EPC and cable companies on standard valuation metrics, which has helped fuel early demand in the unlisted market. The grey market premium for Lumino shares stood at around Rs 38 ahead of the opening, implying a roughly 46% premium over the upper end of the price band, though grey market indicators are unofficial and can shift significantly once formal bidding gets underway and institutional demand becomes clearer.

## Timeline From Here

Following the close of subscription on August 31, the basis of allotment is expected to be finalized around September 1, with refunds and demat credits typically following within a day or two after that. A listing on the BSE and NSE is currently penciled in for September 3, subject to the usual regulatory and exchange processes. As with any IPO, the eventual listing-day performance will depend on how subscription numbers shape up across investor categories over the next few sessions, as well as the broader market mood heading into early September.
