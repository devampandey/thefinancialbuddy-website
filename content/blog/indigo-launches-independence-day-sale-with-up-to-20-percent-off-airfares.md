IndiGo has kicked off its "Happy Independence Day Sale," offering travellers discounts of up to 20 percent on base fares across its domestic and international network, timed to coincide with the run-up to India's Independence Day celebrations.

## Sale Details

The offer is valid on bookings made between August 13 and August 16, 2026, for travel dates spanning August 16 through October 31, 2026 — giving travellers a fairly wide window to plan trips across the upcoming festive and holiday season. To avail the discount, customers need to book at least three days in advance of their travel date and apply the promo code "INDIGO80" at checkout.

The sale is accessible through IndiGo's website, its mobile app, the airline's AI-powered virtual assistant 6Eskai, and select travel partner platforms, making it available across most of the booking channels travellers commonly use.

## Beyond Airfares

IndiGo has bundled in additional savings alongside the base fare discount. Customers can get up to 20 percent off on ancillary offerings including 6E Eats (the airline's in-flight meal service), Fast Forward (priority check-in and boarding), and 6E Seats (seat selection). The airline is also offering Fare Hold starting at Rs 20, allowing travellers to lock in a fare before completing payment, along with IndiGo UpFront upgrades starting from Rs 999.

Loyalty programme members haven't been left out either — IndiGo BluChip members can earn up to 20,000 BluChips on IndiGoStretch bookings made directly through the airline's own channels, for travel between August 24 and September 30, 2026.

## Why the Timing Matters

Airline sales timed around Independence Day have become something of an annual fixture in India's aviation calendar, with carriers looking to capture a wave of domestic leisure travel demand as the long weekend and subsequent festive season approach. For consumers, these promotional windows often represent some of the more attractive fare opportunities of the year, particularly for routes and travel dates that typically see steep price increases closer to the festive months of October and November.

For price-conscious travellers planning trips over the coming months, the combination of fare discounts, ancillary savings and loyalty bonuses gives multiple ways to reduce the overall cost of a trip — though, as with any promotional fare sale, availability on popular routes and peak travel dates is likely to be limited, and travellers looking to lock in the best deals may want to act within the four-day booking window before the offer closes on August 16.
