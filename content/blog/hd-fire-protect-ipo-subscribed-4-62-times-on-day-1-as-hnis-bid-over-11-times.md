HD Fire Protect's initial public offering cleared its full size within hours of opening on Tuesday and closed its first day subscribed 4.62 times, with wealthy individual investors doing most of the heavy lifting.

By the end of day one, the Rs 712 crore issue had received bids for about 8.50 crore shares against 1.84 crore shares on offer, according to exchange data. The issue opened a day after the company raised Rs 213 crore from anchor investors ([see our earlier coverage](/ipo/hd-fire-protect-raises-rs-213-crore-from-anchor-investors-mutual-funds-take-72-of)).

## Category-wise demand

The non-institutional investor (NII) portion led the book on the first day:

- **Non-institutional investors:** 11.37 times
  - Bids above Rs 10 lakh (bNII): 12.51 times
  - Bids between Rs 2 lakh and Rs 10 lakh (sNII): 10.80 times
- **Retail investors:** 3.78 times
- **Qualified institutional buyers (QIBs):** 1.04 times
- **Employees:** 2.93 times

The pattern is worth noting. The issue was only around one time subscribed by late morning, and most of the climb to 4.62 times came in the afternoon as NII and retail bids built up. Institutional investors have so far only just covered their portion. QIB money usually arrives in size on the final day, so that figure is the one to watch on Thursday.

## Grey market signals

Unofficial grey market quotes, which are not regulated and often swing sharply, put the premium at around Rs 91 per share on Tuesday evening. Against the upper price band of Rs 271, that implies a possible listing near Rs 362, about 34% above the issue price. Grey market indications are speculative and can reverse quickly, particularly if broader market sentiment turns.

## What investors are bidding for

Each lot is 55 shares, which works out to about Rs 14,905 at the top of the Rs 258-271 band. The entire issue is an offer for sale by the promoters, so the company itself does not receive any of the proceeds. At the upper end, HD Fire Protect is valued at around Rs 4,750 crore.

## A busy IPO window

HD Fire Protect is one of two issues that opened on Tuesday. The much smaller Rs 42 crore Vaibhav Vyapaar IPO had a far quieter start, subscribed only about 0.19 times by the evening. The contrast underlines how selective investors have become: established names in niche industrial segments are drawing heavy bids, while smaller issues are struggling for attention.

## Key dates

- **Issue closes:** Thursday, October 15
- **Basis of allotment:** October 16
- **Refunds and credit of shares:** October 19
- **Tentative listing on BSE and NSE:** October 21

Early-day subscription numbers often rise sharply over the remaining two days, so the final multiple is likely to look quite different from Tuesday's tally.
