Google has officially launched its Pixel 11 smartphone lineup in India, unveiling four new devices alongside a fresh smartwatch and accessory at its annual "Made by Google" event held overnight. The launch, hosted by comedian and television personality Trevor Noah, brought the new phones to Indian buyers just hours after their global debut.

## The Lineup

The Pixel 11 series comprises four models: the standard Pixel 11, the Pixel 11 Pro, the Pixel 11 Pro XL, and the more premium Pixel 11 Pro Fold. All four devices are powered by Google's newly developed Tensor G6 chipset, which the company has positioned around faster on-device AI processing and deeper integration with its Gemini assistant.

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold stands out as the flagship of the range, featuring a folding 8-inch inner display paired with a 6.5-inch external cover screen. Compared with last year's Pixel 10 Pro Fold, the new Fold is said to be roughly a millimetre thinner and nearly 10 percent lighter, addressing one of the more common criticisms of foldable phones. It is available in Obsidian and Olive colour options.

A notable new hardware feature across the range is "HiLight," an LED ring built into the camera module around the flash. The ring uses colour-coded light patterns to flag priority contacts, provide real-time feedback during hands-free interactions with Gemini, and will eventually support notifications for incoming messages.

## Indian Pricing

In India, the base Pixel 11 will retail starting at Rs 89,999, roughly Rs 10,000 higher than where the Pixel 10 launched a year earlier. The Pixel 11 Pro has been priced at Rs 1,19,999, while the larger Pixel 11 Pro XL comes in at Rs 1,34,999. At the top of the range, the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, in its 512GB storage and 16GB RAM configuration, is priced at Rs 1,86,999, making it comfortably the most expensive device Google has launched in the country to date.

Alongside the phones, Google also introduced the Pixel Watch 5 and a new "Pixel Tag" item tracker, extending its hardware ecosystem further into wearables and accessories, an area where it has been steadily building out a presence to compete with rivals such as Apple and Samsung.

## Why It Matters for Indian Buyers

The price increase across the Pixel 11 range reflects a broader trend among premium smartphone makers passing on higher component and chip development costs to consumers. For Google, the Indian market represents a small but strategically important slice of its overall smartphone business, one where it has been trying to build brand recognition against entrenched players like Samsung and Apple, as well as fast-growing Chinese brands.

Analysts tracking the Indian smartphone market will be watching sell-through numbers for the Pixel 11 closely, given that Google has historically struggled to move meaningful volumes in India relative to its ambitions, despite consistently positive reviews for its camera and software experience. With pricing now pushing further into the premium bracket, the strategy this year appears focused on margin and brand positioning rather than volume-led growth.
