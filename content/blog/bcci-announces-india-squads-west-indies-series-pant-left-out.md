The Board of Control for Cricket in India announced its white-ball squads for the upcoming home series against West Indies on Wednesday evening, along with a revised squad for the men's cricket competition at the Asian Games, in a selection update that carried a mix of continuity at the top and fresh faces further down the order.

## The ODI and T20I Squads

For the ODI leg, the selectors retained a settled top order featuring Shubman Gill as captain alongside Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, Ruturaj Gaikwad and KL Rahul, who was named vice-captain. Dhruv Jurel, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Ravindra Jadeja, Kuldeep Yadav, Prasidh Krishna, Gurnoor Brar, Mohammed Siraj, Yashasvi Jaiswal and Naman Dhir round out the squad, with uncapped pacer Auqib Nabi earning his maiden ODI call-up.

The T20I squad is led by Shreyas Iyer, with Tilak Varma as his deputy. That group includes Abhishek Sharma, Vaibhav Suryavanshi, Ishan Kishan and Sanju Samson as the two designated wicketkeepers, Shivam Dube, Kuldeep Yadav, Axar Patel, Washington Sundar, Ravi Bishnoi, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Arshdeep Singh, Prince Yadav and Mayank Yadav.

India's ODI series against West Indies gets underway in Thiruvananthapuram on September 27, before the action shifts to the shorter format with the T20I leg opening in Lucknow on October 6.

## Pant Overlooked Again

Perhaps the most notable name missing from both white-ball squads is Rishabh Pant, who continues to find himself on the outside looking in in that format despite his standing as one of India's frontline red-ball batters. His absence extends a selection trend that has persisted across recent series and is likely to keep the wicketkeeping conversation in the spotlight ahead of India's next major white-ball assignments. Naman Dhir's call-up, by contrast, rewards a run of domestic and franchise form that selectors have clearly been tracking closely, while Auqib Nabi's inclusion adds fresh pace depth to the bowling unit.

## Asian Games Squad Also Revised

Alongside the West Indies squads, the BCCI updated its squad for the men's cricket competition at the Asian Games, where India will be defending a title on the continental stage. The revision saw Nitish Kumar Reddy withdrawn from the Asian Games party so he can instead feature in the home series against West Indies, a swap that reflects selectors managing workload and availability across two overlapping assignments. Spinner Varun Chakravarthy, who had already been ruled out of contention with a side strain, remains unavailable for the Asian Games, with Vipraj Nigam continuing as his replacement in that squad.

## What It Signals

Taken together, Wednesday's announcements offer an early read on how the selection committee is balancing India's packed white-ball calendar in the coming weeks, with the West Indies series, the Asian Games campaign and the ongoing Irani Cup all drawing from an overlapping pool of contracted players. The continued exclusion of a player of Pant's profile from white-ball plans, even as newer names like Dhir and Nabi break through, suggests the selectors are prepared to back current form and role-specific fits over reputation alone as India builds toward its next limited-overs assignments.
