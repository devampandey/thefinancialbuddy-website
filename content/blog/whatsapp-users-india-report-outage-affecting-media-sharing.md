WhatsApp users across India ran into trouble sending and receiving multimedia content on Monday, with outage-tracking platforms recording a sharp spike in complaints that pointed to a disruption in the app's media-handling infrastructure rather than a complete shutdown of the service.

## What Users Experienced

Reports began surfacing early in the day and climbed steadily before peaking around mid-morning. Affected users described photos, videos, voice notes, stickers and GIFs getting stuck mid-upload or failing to send altogether, even as basic text messaging continued to function for most people. Several users posted screenshots on social media showing repeated loading icons and prompts asking them to retry failed uploads, a pattern consistent with a server-side problem rather than an issue isolated to individual devices or networks.

The disruption was not confined to India. Users in the United Kingdom, France, Brazil, Canada, Mexico, Spain and Colombia also flagged similar problems around the same window, suggesting the underlying cause sat somewhere in WhatsApp's backend media infrastructure rather than with any single country's telecom network. Meta, WhatsApp's parent company, had not issued a detailed public explanation for the disruption at the time reports were compiled.

## Cities Across India Affected

Complaints emerged from users in Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Lucknow and Nagpur, indicating the outage cut across regions rather than being limited to a specific city or telecom circle. For a platform that functions as the default communication tool for both personal and business use across much of India, even a partial disruption to media sharing has an outsized practical impact, from families sharing photos to small businesses sending product images and invoices to customers.

## An Unrelated Wrinkle: Account Reviews

Adding to user confusion, some people also reported seeing account-review or temporary lock messages around the same time as the media outage, though it was not immediately clear whether these notices were connected to the broader technical disruption or were separate, routine enforcement actions unrelated to the day's events. WhatsApp has in recent months stepped up automated account reviews as part of its ongoing effort to curb spam and policy violations, so the overlap in timing does not necessarily imply a shared cause.

## What Affected Users Can Do

For those hit by the disruption, standard troubleshooting steps such as restarting the device or switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data offered relief in some cases but not others, with a number of users reporting that media-sharing problems persisted even after these steps. Given that the fault appeared to originate on WhatsApp's servers rather than on individual phones, repeatedly uninstalling and reinstalling the app is unlikely to resolve the issue any faster; users needing to share time-sensitive files were advised to fall back on email, SMS or another messaging platform until normal service resumes. Outages of this kind, while disruptive, have historically been resolved within hours once WhatsApp's engineering teams identify and roll back the underlying fault.
