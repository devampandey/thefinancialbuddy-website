Wall Street closed out a turbulent week on a positive note Friday, even as all three major US indexes finished lower for the week overall, weighed down by persistent volatility in the bond market and simmering geopolitical tension over Iran.

## Friday's Rebound

The S&P 500 added 0.43% to close at 7,674.37, while the Nasdaq Composite also rose 0.43% to finish at 26,180.45. The Dow Jones Industrial Average was the standout performer, climbing 517.80 points, or 0.98%, to end the session at 53,277.01. Healthcare stocks provided much of the Dow's lift, with heavyweight names among the top gainers on the day.

Despite the single-day rally, the broader picture for the week was less encouraging. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq were on track to snap three-week winning streaks, while the Dow logged its second consecutive weekly decline. Investors have spent the week grappling with a bond market that has refused to settle down, injecting fresh uncertainty into equity trading even as individual sessions swung between sharp losses and strong rebounds.

## Bond Yields Stay in Focus

Treasury yields extended their climb Friday, with the benchmark 10-year note rising more than 3 basis points to 4.734% and the 30-year bond adding a similar amount to reach 5.273%. Elevated long-term borrowing costs have been a persistent drag on risk sentiment throughout the week, as higher yields make bonds more attractive relative to stocks and raise concerns about financing costs across the economy. A Treasury effort earlier in the week to calm the bond market fell short, contributing to one of the sharper down days investors have seen recently before Friday's recovery.

## Iran Pressure Campaign Looms Over Markets

Adding to the uncertain mood, investors are closely watching Washington's escalating economic pressure campaign against Iran. The administration has threatened sweeping consequences for countries that continue trade with Tehran, a stance that has drawn pushback from major trading partners and kept a layer of geopolitical risk hanging over markets already unsettled by the yield swings. Traders are awaiting further clarity on how aggressively the policy will be enforced and what retaliatory steps, if any, follow.

## Bitcoin Extends Its Rally

Away from equities, bitcoin continued its recent surge, extending a rally that has been fueled by a mix of factors including renewed inflows into US spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds and growing expectations that Washington will move to formalize a clearer regulatory framework for digital assets. The cryptocurrency's gains this week stood in contrast to the choppier action in stocks and bonds, underscoring how differently risk appetite has played out across asset classes.

## What's Next

Attention now turns to next week's Jackson Hole Economic Policy Symposium, where the new Federal Reserve chair is scheduled to speak. Markets will be looking for signals on the central bank's approach to interest rates and its stance on independence from political pressure, themes that have taken on added weight given the recent turbulence in long-term yields. Until then, analysts expect markets to remain sensitive to any fresh headlines on both the bond market and the Iran standoff.
