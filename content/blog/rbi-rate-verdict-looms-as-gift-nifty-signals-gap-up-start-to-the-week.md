Indian equity markets are heading into a pivotal week, with the Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) beginning its three-day deliberations today, even as early indicators point to a positive start for domestic indices. GIFT Nifty futures were trading around 150-170 points above the previous close in early trade, suggesting the Nifty 50 could open with a gap-up of more than 100 points. The setup gives investors a cautiously optimistic backdrop as they wait for RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra's rate announcement later in the week.

## RBI's Rate Call Takes Centre Stage

The MPC's three-day meeting is scheduled to run through August 5, with the governor's policy statement due at 10:00 AM IST that day. Most economists tracking the central bank expect the repo rate to be held steady at 5.25 percent, where it has stood since the RBI's last cut of 25 basis points in December 2025. The central bank has kept rates unchanged through three subsequent reviews as it weighs inflation trends against the need to support growth.

By the time the MPC meets this week, it will have access to fresher data than it did in June, including early Q1 FY27 GDP estimates, updated inflation prints, and a clearer sense of how the monsoon has progressed across the country. Developments in global energy markets, particularly around the ongoing West Asia situation, are also likely to factor into the committee's assessment of imported inflation risks. Any signal of a more hawkish stance, even without an actual rate change, could influence how bond yields and rate-sensitive stocks move in the sessions ahead.

## Technical Picture Stays Constructive

On the charts, the Nifty has been consolidating in a relatively narrow band but has continued to print higher highs and higher lows on both daily and weekly timeframes, a pattern technical analysts generally read as a sign that the underlying bullish trend remains intact. The index closed last week near the 24,400 mark, with resistance seen in the 24,500-24,600 zone, which represents the confluence of highs from July and April. A decisive move past that band would mark a breakout from the roughly three-month consolidation range and could open the path toward 24,800 and eventually 25,000 in the coming weeks, according to chart watchers. On the downside, dips are widely being viewed as buying opportunities as long as the broader structure holds.

## Global Cues Add to the Optimism

Overseas markets provided a supportive backdrop heading into the new week. Wall Street closed higher on Friday, with the S&P 500 rising 0.70 percent to end at 7,489.72, the Nasdaq gaining about 1 percent to close at 25,373.85, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average adding 0.53 percent to finish at 52,485.03. The rally was driven largely by strong quarterly results from a major technology company, which reinforced investor confidence in the broader artificial intelligence trade. That strength in US markets, combined with the gap-up signalled by GIFT Nifty, has set a constructive tone for Indian equities as trading begins.

## What Investors Are Watching

Beyond the RBI decision, market participants are also keeping an eye on incoming PMI data, which will offer an early read on manufacturing and services activity for July. Any surprises there, positive or negative, could add volatility around the same window as the rate announcement. With the policy verdict, growth data, and global cues all converging within days of each other, this week is shaping up to be a genuine test of whether the Nifty's recent resilience can translate into a sustained breakout, or whether markets settle into another round of range-bound trade while they digest the RBI's message.
