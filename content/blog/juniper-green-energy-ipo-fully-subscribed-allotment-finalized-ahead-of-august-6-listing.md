The initial public offering of renewable energy developer Juniper Green Energy has crossed its final hurdle before listing, with the basis of allotment finalized today after the issue drew strong interest from institutional investors.

## Deal Details

The Rs 1,800 crore IPO was open for public subscription from July 30 to August 3, with a price band of Rs 214 to Rs 225 per share. The issue was fully subscribed by the time bidding closed, with qualified institutional buyers showing particular enthusiasm for the offer, drawn by the company's portfolio of wind-solar hybrid and firm dispatchable renewable energy, or FDRE, assets. FDRE projects are increasingly in demand from power distribution companies because they can supply electricity on a more predictable, round-the-clock basis than standalone solar or wind plants, which depend on weather conditions.

## Grey Market Signals

Ahead of listing, shares of Juniper Green Energy have been commanding a modest premium in the unlisted grey market, trading at roughly Rs 8 over the upper end of the price band, implying an indicative listing price in the neighbourhood of Rs 233. Grey market premiums are an informal, unregulated gauge of listing-day sentiment and can shift quickly, so investors should treat them as a rough signal rather than a guarantee of listing gains.

## What Happens Next

With allotment finalized today, refunds for investors who did not receive shares are expected to be processed shortly, and shares are scheduled to be credited to successful applicants' demat accounts ahead of the listing. Juniper Green Energy's shares are tentatively set to debut on both the NSE and BSE on August 6.

## Why the Sector Matters

The IPO adds to a busy year for renewable energy listings on Indian exchanges, as the government continues to push for higher clean energy capacity additions and companies look to public markets to fund expansion. Renewable energy issuers have had a mixed track record on listing day in recent years, with investor appetite often depending on the specific technology mix, revenue visibility from long-term power purchase agreements, and broader market conditions at the time of debut. Juniper Green Energy's FDRE-heavy portfolio has been cited by analysts as a differentiating factor relative to some peers that rely more heavily on conventional solar or wind capacity alone.

## A Busy Week for IPO Investors

Juniper Green Energy's listing arrives in the middle of an active primary market week, with other mainboard and SME issues also in various stages of subscription and listing. Investors tracking the broader IPO calendar should note that allotment and listing timelines can shift depending on regulatory processing and market conditions, so it is worth confirming exact dates through official registrar or exchange notifications closer to the listing date.
