Mumbai-based real estate developer Runwal Enterprises opened its initial public offering for subscription on Friday, becoming the latest property player to test investor appetite even as broader markets remain volatile after this week's sharp selloff in financial stocks. The Rs 499.83-crore issue is priced in a band of Rs 290 to Rs 305 per share and will remain open for bidding through September 29.

The offer is structured entirely as a fresh issue of roughly 1.64 crore equity shares, with no offer-for-sale component, meaning the entire proceeds will flow into the company rather than to existing shareholders cashing out. That structure is often read by analysts as a signal that a company intends to use IPO capital for growth rather than to let promoters or early investors exit, and Runwal has said the funds will largely support ongoing and upcoming real estate projects and general corporate purposes.

## Anchor Book Sets the Tone

Ahead of the public opening, Runwal Enterprises raised Rs 148.95 crore from ten anchor investors on Thursday, allotting shares at the top end of the price band, Rs 305 apiece. A strong anchor allocation a day before the public issue opens is typically viewed as an early vote of confidence from institutional investors, and it often shapes sentiment among retail and non-institutional bidders once the issue opens more broadly.

At the upper end of the price band, retail investors need to commit at least Rs 14,945 for a single lot of 49 shares, with allotments in multiples thereafter. The issue reserves half of the net offer for qualified institutional buyers, 15% for non-institutional investors, and the remaining 35% for retail participants.

## Grey Market Signals Modest Listing Gains

In the unofficial grey market, Runwal Enterprises shares were commanding a premium of around Rs 23 on the day of opening, implying an estimated listing price near Rs 328, roughly an 8% gain over the Rs 305 upper price band. Grey market premiums are informal, unregulated indicators and can swing significantly through the bidding window, but the current reading suggests investors are pricing in a reasonably positive, if not spectacular, debut.

## A Realty Developer Betting on Mumbai's Housing Cycle

Incorporated in 2016, Runwal Enterprises has built its business around residential projects spanning affordable, mid-income and luxury segments, alongside commercial spaces, retail malls and educational buildings, with a concentrated presence in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. The company also develops large-format integrated townships, a segment that has drawn renewed investor interest as urban housing demand in India's financial capital has stayed resilient despite broader macroeconomic headwinds.

The IPO lands at a moment when India's real estate sector has been a relative bright spot for capital markets, with several developers turning to public listings this year to fund land acquisition and project execution amid steady residential sales volumes in top metros.

## What Happens Next

Bidding closes on September 29, with share allotment expected to be finalised around September 30. Runwal Enterprises shares are tentatively scheduled to list on both the NSE and BSE on October 5. Investors will be watching subscription trends over the coming days, particularly institutional demand, as an early gauge of how the issue is likely to perform on debut.
