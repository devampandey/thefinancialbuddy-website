India's bowlers were made to toil through a long, sweltering opening day in Colombo as a young Sri Lanka Cricket XI side batted first and reached 363 for 8 by the close of play in the three-day tour match at the Nondescripts Cricket Club Ground. The fixture, India's only warm-up assignment before the two-match Test series against Sri Lanka begins, offered the tourists their first extended look at conditions after a five-week gap since their last competitive outing.

## Openers set the tone

The hosts' top order made the most of a flat surface. Ravindu Rasantha and Nishan Fernando put on a brisk opening stand of 110 runs, giving the home side control from the outset. Fernando looked set for a bigger score, striking freely to reach 66 off just 65 balls, before a mix-up between the wickets ended his innings in a run-out that saw both batters converge at the same end. Rasantha was the more measured of the pair, working his way to 71 off 143 deliveries before he was undone in the air, stepping out to a flighted delivery from Manav Suthar and slicing a lofted drive straight to mid-off.

Further down the order, Sonal Dinusha chipped in with a well-made 52, and a seventh-wicket stand of 64 between Dinusha and Ramesh Mendis added further frustration for the visitors during the middle session.

## India's seamers rusty, spinners strike back

With the bulk of the squad returning from an extended break, India's pace attack looked short of rhythm for large parts of the day, unable to consistently trouble a settled Sri Lankan batting line-up on a pitch offering little for the quicks. It was the spin department that eventually turned the tide. Kuldeep Yadav, playing a central role in India's attack on this tour, finished with figures of 2 for 76, while Ravindra Jadeja and Manav Suthar picked up two wickets apiece as well, sharing the workload and pulling back some of the initiative after the early loss of momentum.

By stumps, Sri Lanka Cricket XI were 363 for 8, with Dilum Sudeera unbeaten on 1 and Kesara Nuwantha not out on 9, leaving India needing early wickets on the second morning to wrap up the innings and get some batting practice of their own before the series proper begins.

## Context for the tour

The tour match carries added significance for India given the composition of the squad named for the Sri Lanka series. Ravindra Jadeja is playing his way back to full fitness after recovering from a tennis elbow injury, while the bowling attack is missing a key frontline option following a late injury setback in the pace department. With Shubman Gill leading the side and KL Rahul as vice-captain, the visitors will be keen to use the remaining two days of this match to sharpen combinations, with several spin options — including Kuldeep Yadav, Suthar and Saransh Jain — all in the mix for the Test series that follows.

Day two in Colombo is expected to be shaped by how quickly India can polish off the Sri Lanka Cricket XI innings and how their top order responds with the bat against a hungry young attack looking to make an impression of its own.
