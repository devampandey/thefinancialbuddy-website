Indian equity benchmarks are set for a positive start on Thursday, August 6, with early indicators pointing to a gap-up open even as global cues remain mixed. GIFT Nifty futures were trading with a premium of roughly 40 points over the previous Nifty close in early trade, suggesting buying interest is likely to carry into the domestic session.

## What Overnight Cues Suggest

Wall Street delivered a strong session overnight, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average closing at a fresh record high, gaining more than 260 points, its fifth consecutive day of gains. The broader S&P 500 slipped marginally, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite ended lower, reflecting some rotation away from high-growth names even as blue-chip industrials rallied. That divergence within US indices is typically watched closely by Indian traders, since it hints at how global risk appetite may be shifting sector by sector rather than broadly.

Asian markets, meanwhile, traded without a clear direction in early Thursday dealing, as investors weighed the record US close against lingering concerns around crude oil prices and geopolitical developments in West Asia. This mixed picture across the region is one reason domestic indices may see a firm open without necessarily sustaining sharp gains through the day.

## Domestic Backdrop

The move comes a day after the Reserve Bank of India held its repo rate steady at 5.25 percent, maintaining a neutral policy stance in line with market expectations, while projecting GDP growth of around 6.7 percent for the current financial year. That decision had already lifted sentiment on Wednesday, with the Sensex closing modestly higher and the Nifty settling just above the 24,600 mark, helped by gains in financials and select metal counters.

Thursday also happens to be a weekly derivatives expiry day, which traders will be watching closely. Expiry sessions often see heightened volatility as positions are rolled over or unwound, and any sharp intraday swings should be read in that context rather than as a signal of a broader trend change.

## What to Watch

Beyond the futures signal, market participants are likely to track a clutch of Q1 corporate earnings still trickling in, movements in Brent crude prices, and any fresh developments on the geopolitical front that could influence oil markets. Foreign portfolio flows remain another swing factor; overseas investors have been net sellers of Indian equities for an extended stretch this year, even as domestic institutional buying has helped cushion the market from sharper corrections.

For retail investors, the key takeaway from a gap-up opening driven largely by global futures is that it reflects overnight positioning rather than a fresh domestic catalyst. Follow-through will depend on how earnings and crude oil prices develop through the trading session, along with the usual bout of expiry-day volatility.
