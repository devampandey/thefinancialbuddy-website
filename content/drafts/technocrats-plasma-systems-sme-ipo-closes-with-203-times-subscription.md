The initial public offering of Technocrats Plasma Systems closed on Tuesday with subscription figures that far outstripped expectations, as the small and medium enterprise issue was booked 203.36 times by the close of bidding at 5:03 pm. The overwhelming response marks one of the stronger showings among the recent wave of SME listings on Indian exchanges and underscores the continued retail and institutional appetite for smaller, niche manufacturing plays even as broader markets stay choppy.

## Subscription breakdown

Demand was led by non-institutional investors, whose portion was subscribed 311.98 times, reflecting the participation of high-net-worth individuals chasing listing gains on a heavily oversubscribed issue. The retail individual investor category was subscribed 176.04 times, while qualified institutional buyers came in at 169.54 times, indicating broad-based interest across all classes of applicants rather than demand concentrated in any single investor segment.

## Issue details

The public issue opened for bidding on August 14 and closed on August 18, spanning three full trading sessions. Technocrats Plasma Systems had set a price band of Rs 125 to Rs 132 per share for the offer, with a minimum lot size of 1,000 shares, translating into a minimum investment of roughly Rs 1.32 lakh at the upper end of the band for retail applicants. The company aimed to raise close to Rs 61 crore through the issue, split across the standard investor allocation categories of 35 percent for retail investors, 50 percent for qualified institutional buyers and 15 percent for non-institutional investors.

## About the company

Incorporated in 1994, Technocrats Plasma Systems is an engineering-focused manufacturer specialising in plasma-based surface treatment and coating solutions used across industrial applications. The company's technology is typically deployed by manufacturers seeking to improve the durability, adhesion and finish quality of components before further processing, a niche but steady demand segment within India's broader industrial and engineering supply chain.

## What happens next

With bidding now closed, the basis of allotment for the issue is expected to be finalised on August 19. Successful applicants can expect shares to be credited to their demat accounts shortly after, with the stock tentatively scheduled to make its listing debut on the BSE SME platform on August 21. Investors who did not receive an allotment will see refunds processed around the same time.

## Why the demand matters

The scale of oversubscription on an SME-platform issue of this size is notable given the mixed mood in India's broader primary market this week, where several other small-cap offerings have struggled to draw even full subscription on their final day. Analysts tracking the SME IPO space have pointed to a pattern this year in which niche industrial and engineering companies with clear, understandable business models continue to attract disproportionate retail interest, even as investors turn more selective on issues from sectors seen as more crowded or richly valued. Whether that enthusiasm translates into a strong listing-day pop for Technocrats Plasma Systems will become clear once the stock begins trading later this week.
