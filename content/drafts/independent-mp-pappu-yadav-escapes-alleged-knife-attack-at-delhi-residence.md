A tense scene unfolded at the Delhi residence of independent Lok Sabha MP Pappu Yadav on Sunday, after an unidentified man allegedly attempted to attack him with a knife in the middle of a press conference. The Purnia MP emerged unharmed from the incident, but it has reignited questions about the security arrangements around sitting parliamentarians and has already become a talking point across Bihar's political circles.

## How the Incident Unfolded

According to accounts from those present, Yadav was addressing reporters at his residence when a man in the gathering suddenly moved toward him. Several reports describe the man brandishing a knife, while others note that a shoe was also thrown in Yadav's direction during the commotion, with the MP himself later asserting that his attacker had intended to kill him with a blade. Supporters and aides who were present reacted quickly, surrounding the man, restraining him, and, by multiple accounts, roughing him up before handing him over to Delhi Police personnel who arrived at the scene.

Yadav was not injured in the scuffle and continued to address the assembled media shortly after order was restored. Delhi Police confirmed that a man had been detained in connection with the incident, though at the time of reporting, authorities had not released further details about the accused's identity, his background, or any stated motive for the alleged attack.

## Yadav Alleges a Wider Conspiracy

In the aftermath, Yadav told reporters that he believed the incident was not an isolated act but part of a broader conspiracy targeting him. He said he had previously filed multiple complaints with the relevant authorities flagging threats to his personal safety, and suggested that Sunday's episode should be viewed in that context. He called for a thorough police investigation into who was behind the man's presence at his residence and what, if anything, motivated the attempted attack.

Yadav, who represents Purnia as an independent MP with longstanding ties to Bihar's political landscape, has been a vocal and often combative figure in national politics for years, and has previously spoken about perceived threats against him. His allegations of a conspiracy will likely add pressure on Delhi Police to move quickly on the investigation and to clarify the identity and intent of the detained individual.

## A Story Still Developing

As of now, official confirmation of the finer details, including whether the object involved was conclusively a knife, remains awaited, with different eyewitness and media accounts offering slightly varying descriptions of exactly what transpired in the chaotic moments of the scuffle. A formal statement from Delhi Police, along with charges if any are filed against the detained man, is expected in the coming days.

The episode adds to a string of recent incidents involving threats or attacks on Indian politicians and is likely to keep security protocols for MPs, particularly those without formal central security cover, under scrutiny in the days ahead.
