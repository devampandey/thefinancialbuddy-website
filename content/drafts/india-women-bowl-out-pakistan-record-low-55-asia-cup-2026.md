India Women delivered one of their most dominant performances in the India-Pakistan cricket rivalry on Saturday, bowling out Pakistan for just 55 runs in 18.1 overs during their Group A fixture at the Women's Asia Cup 2026 in Dubai. The result marks Pakistan's lowest-ever total in Women's T20 International cricket, eclipsing their previous low of 56, which had also come against another opponent in Dubai back in 2024.

## A Collapse From the Start

Pakistan captain Fatima Sana won the toss and opted to bat first at the Dubai International Cricket Stadium, a decision that unravelled almost immediately as India's bowlers found early rhythm and never let up. Only two Pakistan batters managed to reach double figures: Muneeba Ali, who top-scored with 13, and Shawaal Zulfiqar, who made 14. The rest of the batting order fell in quick succession, with India's spin and seam options combining to keep the innings pinned down throughout.

Shree Charani and Prema Rawat were the standout bowlers for India, each finishing with three wickets from their full quota of four overs while conceding just seven and nine runs respectively. Their tight, disciplined spells gave Pakistan's batters little room to rotate the strike or build any momentum. Indian captain Harmanpreet Kaur rotated her bowling attack effectively, ensuring wickets fell at regular intervals and any hint of a partnership was snuffed out before it could develop.

## Extending an Unbeaten Run

The win added to what has already been a commanding campaign for India in this edition of the Asia Cup. Having already secured their place in the semi-finals following earlier group-stage victories over Thailand and Hong Kong, India used Saturday's match to further cement their position atop the Group A points table, taking their winning streak in the tournament to three matches in a row.

For Pakistan, the defeat is a sobering setback in what was already being billed as a high-stakes clash given the intense rivalry between the two sides. The scale of the collapse — bowled out inside 19 overs for a score lower than many T20 totals posted in the powerplay alone — is likely to prompt scrutiny of both the batting approach and the decision to bat first on a surface that appeared to offer early assistance to the bowlers.

## What It Means Going Forward

With qualification for the knockout stage already secured, India will now turn their attention to fine-tuning their combinations before the business end of the tournament begins. The comprehensive nature of Saturday's win, achieved without needing to rely on a single standout batting effort in the chase, will offer the team management further evidence of the depth in their bowling unit heading into the semi-finals.

For Pakistan, the tournament is far from over, but the manner of this defeat means the side will need a swift turnaround in form and confidence to salvage momentum from the group stage. The match adds another chapter to a rivalry that continues to draw outsized attention whenever the two neighbours meet on the cricket field, regardless of the format or the tournament stakes.
