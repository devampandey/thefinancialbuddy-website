Tempsens Instruments (India) Ltd, a thermal engineering and specialised cable manufacturer, opened its initial public offering for subscription on Thursday, seeking to raise Rs 650 crore in one of the week's key mainboard listings.

## Issue Details

The book-built issue comprises a fresh issue worth Rs 95 crore and an offer for sale of Rs 555 crore by existing shareholders, at a price band of Rs 285 to Rs 300 per share. The subscription window opened Thursday and is scheduled to close on August 24, giving investors a five-day window to bid for shares.

Ahead of the public offering, the company raised more than Rs 194 crore from anchor investors, an early indicator of institutional appetite for the issue. Based on the current schedule, allotment is expected to be finalised around August 25, with shares likely to list on both the BSE and NSE around August 28.

## About the Company

Incorporated in 1990, Tempsens Instruments designs and manufactures custom temperature sensors, industrial heating solutions and engineered cables that serve process industries ranging from steel and cement to power generation and petrochemicals. The company has built a niche in thermal engineering equipment, an industrial segment that tends to see steady demand tied to capital expenditure cycles in core manufacturing sectors rather than the more volatile consumer-facing parts of the economy.

Because a majority of the issue size is structured as an offer for sale, a large share of the proceeds will go to selling shareholders rather than into the company's own balance sheet, with only the fresh issue portion of Rs 95 crore adding new capital for the business. Investors evaluating the offer will typically weigh this structure alongside the company's growth plans and existing debt levels before deciding on subscription levels.

## Part of a Busy IPO Week

Tempsens Instruments' listing arrives amid a packed primary market calendar. Gaja Alternative Asset Management's Rs 550 crore IPO was also in its subscription window this week, while several SME-segment offers, including Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds and Mopshop Distribution, were open for bidding alongside mainboard issues such as Shankesh Jewellers and Sunshine Pictures on their final subscription day. The clustering of offerings reflects a broader trend of Indian companies rushing to tap public markets during favourable windows, even as investor appetite has varied significantly across recent listings depending on sector and pricing.

## What Investors Are Watching

As with most new listings, market participants will track grey market activity and subscription figures across retail, non-institutional and qualified institutional buyer categories over the coming days as early signals of demand. Strong anchor investor participation ahead of the opening is often viewed as a preliminary vote of confidence, though final listing-day performance depends on broader market conditions closer to the scheduled debut in late August.

With India's primary market continuing to see a steady stream of new offerings through 2026, Tempsens Instruments' listing will be closely watched as a bellwether for appetite toward industrial and engineering-focused issuers, a category that has had a mixed track record compared with more heavily subscribed consumer and financial services listings this year.
