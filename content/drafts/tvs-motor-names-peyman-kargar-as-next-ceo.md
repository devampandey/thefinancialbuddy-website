TVS Motor Company announced on Friday that Peyman Kargar, currently President of its International Business division, will take over as Chief Executive Officer effective January 27, 2027, succeeding K N Radhakrishnan, who has led the two-wheeler maker since 2008.

The transition marks one of the more significant leadership changes among India's major automakers this year, coming at a time when domestic two-wheeler manufacturers are increasingly looking beyond home markets for their next leg of growth.

## Who Is Peyman Kargar

Kargar joined TVS Motor in March 2025 after spending more than three decades in the global automotive industry across Europe, Asia and the Middle East. His prior roles included senior leadership positions at Nissan's luxury brand Infiniti and at Datsun, giving him deep exposure to both premium and mass-market vehicle segments across diverse geographies.

Since joining TVS, Kargar has been credited with expanding the company's overseas footprint. International markets now account for around 29 percent of the company's total sales volume, a segment that has reportedly been growing at roughly 33 percent, well ahead of the pace of domestic sales growth.

## An Orderly Transition

Radhakrishnan, who has been at the helm for close to two decades, will continue to lead the company through the handover period before stepping down when Kargar formally takes charge in early 2027. The extended runway between the announcement and the actual transition is intended to ensure continuity in strategy and operations during the changeover, a common practice among Indian conglomerates managing generational or leadership shifts at flagship businesses.

The company's board has framed the appointment as a natural evolution of its succession planning, pointing to Kargar's track record in scaling international operations as the key rationale behind elevating an internal executive rather than conducting an external search.

## What It Signals for TVS Motor's Strategy

Kargar's elevation suggests the company intends to lean further into global expansion, product innovation and premiumisation as it competes not just with domestic rivals but also with international two-wheeler and mobility players entering emerging markets. His mandate as CEO is expected to include deepening TVS Motor's presence in developed economies, alongside continuing to build out its electric vehicle lineup domestically.

The auto industry has been watching succession moves closely this year as several legacy Indian manufacturers navigate leadership transitions amid an industry-wide shift toward electrification and software-defined vehicles. TVS Motor's decision to hand the reins to an executive with an international mobility background, rather than a purely domestic operations leader, indicates the company sees its next growth chapter being written substantially outside India's borders.

Investors and analysts will now watch for any strategic recalibration under the incoming leadership, particularly around capital allocation between domestic EV investments and overseas expansion in the run-up to the formal transition in 2027.
