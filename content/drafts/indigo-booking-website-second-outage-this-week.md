IndiGo's website and mobile app ran into a fresh bout of technical trouble on Friday, the second such disruption to hit India's largest airline within a single week, leaving travellers unable to book tickets, complete web check-in or reliably access their accounts.

## What Went Wrong

The airline said in a service advisory that it was experiencing "intermittent issues" with its core reservation platform, warning that some customers might struggle to reach its online services. During the outage, users attempting to search for flights or complete bookings on IndiGo's website were met with a "no data available" message, while others reported being unable to log into the mobile app at all. On social media, passengers described failed booking attempts, stalled web check-ins, and payments that appeared to go through without any confirmation ever arriving -- leaving many uncertain whether their transactions had actually succeeded.

IndiGo acknowledged the problem publicly and said its technical teams, working alongside an external technology partner, were treating the fix as a priority. The airline has not said what caused the glitch or named the vendor involved in its reservation infrastructure, and by the time of writing had not given a timeline for full restoration.

## Not a One-Off

The bigger concern for passengers is the recurrence: this is the second time this week that IndiGo's core booking system has broken down. Just two days earlier, the airline reported a similar "intermittent" problem affecting the same reservation platform, raising the question of whether Friday's disruption reflects a lingering, unresolved issue rather than an isolated technical hiccup.

## Why the Stakes Are High

IndiGo is not just any carrier -- it commands roughly two-thirds of India's domestic aviation market, based on July's traffic figures, making it the dominant gateway through which most Indian air travellers book, check in for and manage their flights online. When its digital backbone falters, the ripple effects are felt disproportionately across the country's flying public, from leisure travellers trying to lock in festive-season fares to business travellers needing same-day changes. Repeated outages within a short window also raise uncomfortable questions for an airline that has increasingly pushed customers toward digital self-service channels -- web check-in, app-based rebooking and online payment -- as a way to cut costs and reduce airport counter congestion.

## What Passengers Should Do

Until IndiGo confirms the issue is fully resolved, travellers with imminent bookings are advised to keep screenshots of any payment confirmations, monitor the airline's official social media channels for updates, and consider calling customer support or visiting an airport counter directly if a booking or check-in cannot be completed online. For an airline whose scale makes reliability a systemic concern rather than a customer-service footnote, back-to-back outages in the same week are likely to keep pressure on IndiGo to explain -- and fix -- what is going wrong with its digital backbone.
