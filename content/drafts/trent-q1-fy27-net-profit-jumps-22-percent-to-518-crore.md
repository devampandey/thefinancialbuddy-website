Trent Ltd, the Tata Group-owned retail chain behind Westside and Zudio, reported a strong start to fiscal year 2027, with consolidated net profit rising 21.98% year-on-year to Rs 518.07 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, up from Rs 424.70 crore in the same period last year.

## Revenue and Profitability

Consolidated revenue from operations for the June quarter came in at Rs 5,754.71 crore, an increase of roughly 17.8% over the Rs 4,883.48 crore reported in the year-ago quarter. Total consolidated income, which includes other income streams beyond core operations, rose to Rs 5,784.54 crore from Rs 4,924.07 crore a year earlier.

Total consolidated expenses also rose in line with the scale-up in business, climbing to Rs 5,082.96 crore for the quarter from Rs 4,368.59 crore in Q1 FY26. On a per-share basis, both basic and diluted earnings per share stood at Rs 9.73 for the quarter, up from Rs 8.06 in the corresponding period last year. It's worth noting that Trent issued a bonus share in the ratio of one share for every two held during the quarter, and the company has adjusted prior-period EPS figures to reflect this bonus issue for a like-for-like comparison.

## Leadership and Governance Changes

Alongside the results, Trent announced a change at the top of its internal audit function. Varsha Agarwal, who has more than two decades of experience in finance and accounts and previously led the company's Internal Financial Controls function, will take over as Head of Internal Audit effective September 1, 2026. She replaces Ratul Neogi, who is set to retire on reaching superannuation on August 31, 2026.

The company also flagged an upcoming change in its statutory auditor. Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP, which has served as Trent's auditor for two consecutive five-year terms, will step down after the company's 75th Annual General Meeting in 2027, as mandated by auditor rotation norms. The board has recommended B S R & Co. LLP as the successor, for a term running from the 75th AGM in 2027 through the 80th AGM in 2032.

## Context Behind the Numbers

Trent's results also referenced an exceptional item of Rs 26.11 crore tied to the transition to India's four new unified labour codes, which consolidated 29 separate pieces of labour legislation and took effect from November 21, 2025. The company noted that its core business remains retailing and trading of merchandise, with no separately reportable business segments, meaning its fashion, grocery and beauty formats are all folded into a single consolidated performance picture.

The quarter's growth comes at a time when several other Indian retailers and FMCG names have flagged uneven consumer demand, making Trent's double-digit revenue and profit growth notable. Investors and analysts will likely watch subsequent quarters closely to see whether the momentum in value-format retail, led by chains like Zudio, continues to offset any softness in premium retail spending.
