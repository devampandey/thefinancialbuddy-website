East Zone were crowned champions of the 2026 Duleep Trophy on Thursday, defeating South Zone on first-innings lead in the final at the MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai to end a 13-year wait for the title, their first since the 2012-13 season.

Captain Ishan Kishan set the tone for East Zone's triumph with a dominant double century, anchoring a mammoth first-innings total of 708. He was well supported by Kumar Kushagra, who made 101, Shikhar Mohan, who contributed 85, and Abhimanyu Easwaran, who chipped in with 72, as East Zone batted South Zone out of the contest from the outset.

## Tilak Varma's Heartbreak

South Zone, replying to that daunting total, were bowled out for 336, handing East Zone a first-innings lead of 372 runs that ultimately proved decisive. The innings produced a moment of individual heartbreak for South Zone captain Tilak Varma, India's T20I vice-captain, who fell for 99 while attempting to press home an advantage for his side, becoming the last man dismissed as South Zone's innings folded. Devdutt Padikkal was among the other contributors for South Zone, top-scoring with 62, but no other batter was able to convert a start into a substantial score against a disciplined East Zone attack led by Mohammed Shami and Mukesh Kumar, who picked up two and three wickets respectively.

## East Zone Seal The Result

With a massive first-innings cushion in hand, East Zone declared their second innings on 388 for 7, with Md Kounain Quraishi remaining unbeaten on 116, comfortably beyond the threat of a South Zone comeback. The match ultimately ended without an outright result on the field, with East Zone declared winners on the strength of their commanding first-innings advantage, a common method of settling drawn first-class fixtures in Indian domestic cricket when time runs out for a definitive finish.

## A Statement Domestic Season

The victory represents a significant statement for East Zone's domestic cricket set-up, which has often played second fiddle to the more fancied South, West and North zones in recent editions of the tournament. Kishan's captaincy and batting form, alongside contributions from a mix of experienced campaigners and emerging talents, will be seen as encouraging signs for a zone looking to reassert itself on the national stage.

For South Zone, the defeat ends their bid for back-to-back Duleep Trophy titles and will raise questions about their middle-order depth beyond Varma, even as his own near-century performance drew widespread praise for its fighting quality under pressure. With several contracted India players featuring across both line-ups, the final also offered national selectors a useful form guide ahead of the upcoming domestic and international season, as India's cricketing calendar moves toward a busy white-ball and Test schedule in the coming months.
