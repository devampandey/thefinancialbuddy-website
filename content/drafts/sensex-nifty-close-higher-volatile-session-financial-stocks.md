India's benchmark equity indices closed higher on Thursday after a choppy session, as strength in financial stocks helped offset pressure from elevated crude oil prices and cautious global cues. The Nifty 50 settled at 23,477.80, up 46.30 points, or 0.20%, while the BSE Sensex closed at 74,902.59, higher by 138.36 points, or 0.19%. The Bank Nifty index outperformed, ending the session at 56,471.95, up 176.40 points, or 0.31%.

## A Volatile Day On Dalal Street

Trading was choppy through the session, with indices swinging between gains and losses before financial stocks helped tip the balance firmly into positive territory by the close. Domestic markets had opened on a mixed note, tracking negative global cues after Brent crude futures pushed above the $100-a-barrel mark for the first time in months, driven by escalating tensions around the Strait of Hormuz amid the ongoing conflict between the United States and Iran.

## Sectoral Trends Diverge Sharply

Sectoral performance was mixed through the day. Financial services stocks provided the strongest support to the broader market, helping lift both the Sensex and Bank Nifty into positive territory. In contrast, metal, pharma, auto and energy stocks remained under pressure for much of the session, reflecting concerns over input costs and demand as crude prices climbed. Among sectoral indices on the National Stock Exchange, IT stocks were among the weaker performers, extending recent underperformance, while oil and gas counters were comparatively resilient given the run-up in crude benchmarks.

## Crude Prices Add A Layer Of Caution

The jump in oil prices has become a key swing factor for Indian markets in recent sessions, given India's heavy reliance on imported crude. With the Indian crude basket also trending sharply higher, investors are watching closely for any pass-through impact on inflation and the rupee, both of which have implications for the Reserve Bank of India's policy stance in the months ahead. The rupee has come under modest pressure through the week as oil prices climbed, adding another variable for traders to track alongside the equity market's own swings.

## What's Next For Investors

Market participants said the day's gains, while modest, suggested some resilience in domestic sentiment despite a difficult global backdrop, with foreign flows, crude price movements and the unfolding Middle East situation likely to remain the dominant themes for Indian markets in the near term. Investors are also keeping an eye on preparations for the BRICS Summit in New Delhi later this week, along with a steady stream of corporate earnings, order-win announcements and IPO activity that have kept stock-specific action brisk even as the headline indices moved in a relatively narrow range.

With volatility elevated and global crude markets in flux, analysts said stock selection and sector rotation were likely to matter more than broad index direction in the sessions ahead, particularly for sectors such as aviation, paints and tyres that are directly sensitive to swings in crude oil prices.
