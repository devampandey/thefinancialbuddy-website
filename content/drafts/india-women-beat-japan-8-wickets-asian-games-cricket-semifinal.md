India's women's cricket team began their title defence at the Asian Games 2026 in emphatic fashion on Friday, thrashing hosts Japan by 8 wickets in the quarterfinal to book a place in the semifinals with more than 10 overs to spare.

Playing at the Korogi Athletic Park in Aichi, the contest was over almost as soon as it began. Japan, batting first, were bowled out for a paltry 57 runs in 19.5 overs as India's bowling attack ran through the home side's line-up with little resistance. Left-arm spinner Shree Charani was the standout performer, returning figures of 4 for 10 from her four overs to spearhead the collapse.

## A One-Sided Chase

Chasing a modest target of 58, India's batters made light work of the run chase. Shafali Verma set the tone with aggressive strokeplay from the outset, finding the boundary repeatedly alongside wicketkeeper-batter Richa Ghosh as India raced to victory well inside the 10-over mark. The margin of victory, eight wickets with overs to spare, underlined the gulf in class between the two sides in what was also the first-ever meeting between the Indian and Japanese women's national cricket teams.

## Defending Champions Off to a Strong Start

India travelled to Japan as the gold medal holders from the previous edition of the Asian Games and as the highest-ranked team in the eight-nation women's cricket competition at this year's Games. The team, led by Harmanpreet Kaur, arrived in Aichi-Nagoya on the back of winning a record-extending eighth Women's Asia Cup title, adding to the sense that India entered this tournament as clear favourites to retain their crown.

Friday's result does little to dispel that expectation. A comprehensive win in the opening knockout match, achieved with several batters and bowlers barely tested, gives India both momentum and a chance to manage workloads heading into the knockout rounds that follow.

## What's Next

With the quarterfinal win, India progress to the semifinal stage of the tournament, though the exact opponent and schedule will depend on the results of the remaining quarterfinal fixtures being played across the day. The broader Asian Games 2026 competition officially opens with its ceremony on September 19, meaning India's cricket campaign is already a day ahead of the Games' formal start, a scheduling quirk common to multi-discipline events where certain sports begin fixtures before the opening ceremony to fit a crowded calendar.

For a squad blending seasoned campaigners like Harmanpreet Kaur and Smriti Mandhana with emerging talents such as Shree Charani, Friday's dominant performance offers an early marker of the depth India will be relying on as they chase back-to-back Asian Games gold in women's cricket.
