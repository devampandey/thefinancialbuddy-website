Saikhom Mirabai Chanu added the one major multi-sport medal missing from her collection on Wednesday, winning silver in the women's 49kg weightlifting event at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games 2026 and ending a 28-year wait for an Indian podium finish in the sport at the Games.

## A Career Milestone

Chanu, the Tokyo 2020 Olympic silver medallist, lifted a total of 198kg, combining 87kg in the snatch with 111kg in the clean and jerk, to secure second place on the podium. The result completes a rare sweep for the 31-year-old across the sport's biggest multi-discipline stages: she has previously won gold at the Commonwealth Games, silver at the Olympics, and medals at the World Championships, and an Asian Games medal had been the one notable gap in that record heading into these Games.

For India as a whole, the medal carries additional weight. It marks the country's first podium finish in Asian Games weightlifting in 28 years, a gap that reflects how competitive the sport has become across Asia, particularly against traditional powerhouses such as China and North Korea.

## How the Final Played Out

North Korea's Ri Song Gum, a two-time reigning world champion, lived up to her billing at the top of the field, completing a hat-trick of Asian Games titles with a commanding total of 215kg, made up of a 94kg snatch and a 121kg clean and jerk. Indonesia's Luluk Diana Tri Wijayana took bronze with a total of 191kg. Chanu's 198kg total was enough to hold off the Indonesian lifter and secure the silver, even as she finished well behind Ri's dominant winning mark.

## Part of a Strong Day for India

Chanu's medal came on a packed day for the Indian contingent at the Games, which also saw shooters add to the country's medal tally with bronze finishes in both the men's and women's skeet team events. Anantjeet Singh Naruka, Mairaj Ahmed Khan and Bhavtegh Singh Gill took bronze in the men's competition, while Raiza Dhillon, Parinaaz Dhaliwal and Maheshwari Chauhan did the same in the women's event. India's women's kabaddi team also kept its campaign on track with a 37-23 win over Iran, while the men's badminton team and women's hockey team had further medal-round matches scheduled through the day.

## Why It Matters

Beyond the individual achievement, Chanu's medal is likely to be seen as a boost for Indian weightlifting at a time when the sport has struggled to produce consistent international results outside of her own performances. With a fuller weightlifting contingent still to compete in the coming days, Indian officials will be hoping Wednesday's result signals broader momentum rather than a standalone highlight.
