The initial public offering of Milky Mist Dairy Food, the direct-to-consumer dairy brand, closed out its subscription window on Thursday with a marked surge in demand across investor categories, capping a three-day bidding period for the Rs 1,553-crore issue.

## Subscription Numbers Climb on Final Day

By early afternoon on the last day of bidding, the issue had been subscribed 7.58 times overall, a sharp jump from the more tepid response seen on the opening two days. Non-institutional investors, typically high-net-worth individuals bidding for larger lot sizes, led the charge with subscription levels reaching 15.25 times their allotted quota. Qualified institutional buyers came in at 6.69 times, while the retail portion, reserved for individual investors bidding smaller amounts, was subscribed 4.79 times.

The pattern is a familiar one in India's IPO market this year, where issues have frequently seen demand concentrated in the final hours of bidding as institutional and high-net-worth investors wait for price discovery and grey market signals to firm up before committing capital.

## Issue Details

The Milky Mist Dairy Food IPO opened for subscription on August 11 and closed on August 13, with a price band of Rs 133 to Rs 140 per share. The company, known for its cheese, paneer, ghee and other dairy-based products sold through both retail and food-service channels, is looking to use the proceeds to fund capacity expansion and strengthen its balance sheet as it competes in India's increasingly crowded packaged dairy and dairy-alternative space.

Milky Mist's listing comes at a time when India's primary market has remained active through much of August, with several IPOs from sectors ranging from auto components to logistics either recently listed or currently in the subscription pipeline. The dairy sector specifically has drawn investor interest as rising incomes and shifting consumption patterns push more Indian households toward branded, packaged dairy products over unbranded alternatives.

## What Investors Will Watch Next

With subscription now closed, the next milestones for Milky Mist will be the finalisation of share allotment, followed by a stock market debut in the days ahead. Analysts tracking the grey market and overall subscription trends will be parsing the final day's investor mix closely, given that the sharp late surge in non-institutional and retail demand often signals how a stock is likely to be received once trading begins.

Whether the strong final-day numbers translate into a robust listing-day pop will depend on broader market conditions in the run-up to listing, as well as how the stock is priced relative to demand across the anchor and institutional books. For now, the closing subscription figures suggest healthy overall interest in an issue that started more modestly over its first two days of bidding.
