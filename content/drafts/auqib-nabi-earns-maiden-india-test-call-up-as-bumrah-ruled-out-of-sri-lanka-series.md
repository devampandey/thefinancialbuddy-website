Jammu and Kashmir fast bowler Auqib Nabi has been named as a replacement for Jasprit Bumrah in India's Test squad for the upcoming tour of Sri Lanka, earning his maiden call-up to the senior national side. The selection committee's move comes after Bumrah failed to make a full recovery from the left knee injury he picked up during India's ODI series against England earlier this year.

## Bumrah Sidelined Again

Bumrah's fitness has been a recurring concern for India's team management over the past year, with the pace spearhead managing his workload carefully across formats. His absence from the two-Test series in Sri Lanka means India will once again have to navigate a red-ball assignment without their premier fast bowler, placing greater responsibility on the rest of the pace attack to lead the charge with the new ball.

## Nabi's Road to the Top

The 27-year-old Nabi has built his case for national selection through consistent performances on the domestic circuit. Over the last two Ranji Trophy seasons, he has picked up more than 100 wickets, including around 60 scalps in the 2025-26 season alone, numbers that made him difficult for selectors to ignore. He further strengthened his claim with a recent stint for the India A side on their tour of Sri Lanka, where he took six wickets across two first-class matches, effectively auditioning on the same tracks India's senior team will now play on.

His inclusion continues a broader trend of India's selectors looking beyond the usual metropolitan strongholds for pace bowling talent, with players from Jammu and Kashmir increasingly finding representation at the highest level of the sport.

## Series Details

The opening Test between India and Sri Lanka is scheduled to be played at the Galle International Stadium starting August 15, a venue historically known for offering assistance to spinners as matches progress. The second and final Test of the series will be held at the Sinhalese Sports Club (SSC) in Colombo from August 23. Both venues carry historical significance in India-Sri Lanka cricketing contests and will test the depth of India's bowling unit in Bumrah's continued absence.

For Nabi, the tour represents an opportunity to translate domestic dominance into an international debut, while for India's team management, it is another test of the depth built into the pace bowling reserves as they continue to manage Bumrah's workload with an eye on the longer term.
