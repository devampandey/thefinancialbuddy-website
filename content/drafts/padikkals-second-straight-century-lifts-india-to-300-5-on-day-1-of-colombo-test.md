India ended the first day of the second Test against Sri Lanka at the Sinhalese Sports Club Ground in Colombo on a strong note, reaching 300 for 5 at stumps on Sunday, powered by a fluent century from Devdutt Padikkal. The innings arrives with India already 1-0 up in the series after their commanding 165-run win in the series opener at Galle, and it gives the tourists a firm platform to press for a series-clinching result.

## Padikkal Continues Hot Streak

Padikkal, who scored a century in the first Test as well, carried that form straight into the second match, compiling 117 before falling. His innings was built around two substantial partnerships: a 120-run stand with captain Shubman Gill, who made a composed 50, and a further 74 runs added with Ravindra Jadeja. Padikkal's ability to rotate strike while absorbing pressure from Sri Lanka's pace attack was central to India taking control of the opening day.

The middle order did not have things entirely their own way, however. Sri Lanka's quicks, led by Lahiru Kumara, tested India's batters with short-pitched bowling through the middle session. Kumara struck Rishabh Pant on the wrist with a short delivery, forcing the wicketkeeper-batter to retire hurt, and later found Jadeja's forearm with a similar delivery before eventually dismissing him. Jadeja was bowled shouldering arms in the final over of the day, a fittingly tense end to a session that had otherwise belonged to India.

## Sri Lanka Bowlers Fight Back Late

Asitha Fernando was the pick of the Sri Lankan bowlers, finishing the day with figures of 3 for 46 and giving the home side something to build on heading into day two. Kumara and Keshara Nuwantha picked up one wicket apiece as Sri Lanka worked through India's order after the big opening stands. Despite the wickets, the day's balance of play tilted clearly toward the visitors, who lost only two wickets after tea to close on a healthy total.

At the close of play, debutant Saransh Jain was unbeaten on 4 and Dhruv Jurel was not out on 7, with both batters tasked with resuming the innings on day two and pushing India's total further before the bowlers get a chance to attack Sri Lanka's top order.

## Series Context

The result at Galle, where India bowled Sri Lanka out for a heavy defeat, has put the hosts under pressure to respond on home soil in Colombo. A strong batting performance on day one gives India selectors and team management the platform to dictate terms of the match from the outset, with the bowling attack still to come into play once Sri Lanka bat. With three days still to play, India will be aiming to convert this position into a substantial first-innings lead.
