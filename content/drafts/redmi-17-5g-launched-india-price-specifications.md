Xiaomi launched the Redmi 17 5G in India on Thursday, positioning the smartphone around long battery life and everyday reliability for budget-conscious buyers. The device starts at Rs 23,999 for the 6GB+128GB variant, with an 8GB+128GB option priced at Rs 26,999.

Sales are set to begin on September 10 through Amazon India, Xiaomi's own mi.com platform, and the company's retail and offline stores.

## Battery and display

The standout feature of the Redmi 17 5G is its 7,900mAh battery, paired with 45W wired fast charging and reverse wired charging support, aimed at users who prioritise endurance over slimness. The phone's 6.9-inch display uses an HD+ LCD panel with a 120Hz adaptive refresh rate and peak brightness of up to 825 nits in high-brightness mode.

The screen carries TUV Rheinland certifications for low blue light, flicker-free operation and circadian-friendly viewing, along with Gorilla Glass 7i protection. The device also includes dual stereo speakers for media consumption.

## Performance and cameras

Powering the Redmi 17 5G is Qualcomm's 4-nanometre Snapdragon 4 Gen 5 chipset, clocked up to 2.4GHz, running Xiaomi's HyperOS 3 software on top. On the camera front, the phone gets a 50-megapixel AI-enabled dual rear camera setup with night, portrait, HDR and document modes, plus an 8-megapixel front camera with a soft-light ring for selfies and video calls.

Additional features include an IP64 rating for splash and dust resistance, a side-mounted fingerprint scanner, AI face unlock, microSD expansion of up to 2TB, and a 3.5mm headphone jack — a feature increasingly rare on newer smartphones.

## Design and colour options

The Redmi 17 5G will be available in three colourways: Absolute Black, Endless Blue and Eternal Orange, with a vegan leather finish on select variants aimed at giving the budget device a more premium feel.

## Why it matters

The launch adds to Xiaomi's push to defend its position in India's competitive budget and mid-range 5G smartphone segment, where battery life and display quality have become key differentiators as brands compete on value rather than just price. With a large battery, a smooth 120Hz display and a capable mid-tier chipset, the Redmi 17 5G is aimed squarely at cost-conscious Indian buyers upgrading from older 4G devices or looking for a dependable daily driver without paying premium prices.
