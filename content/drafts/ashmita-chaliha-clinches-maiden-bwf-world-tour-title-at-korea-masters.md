Indian shuttler Ashmita Chaliha won her first BWF World Tour title on Sunday, coming from behind to beat China's Han Qianxi 14-21, 21-14, 21-14 in the women's singles final of the Korea Masters Super 300 tournament in Asan, South Korea.

## A Fightback in Asan

The 26-year-old from Assam dropped the opening game after Han pulled away from a level 11-11 scoreline to close it out 21-14. Chaliha responded in the second game with sharper attacking play and tighter defence, building an 11-8 lead at the interval before extending it to seal the game 21-14 and force a decider.

The final game was tightly contested through the opening stretch, with Han holding an 11-9 edge at the mid-game break. Chaliha then lifted her intensity, working her way to a 12-11 lead with a series of precise winners before pulling clear at 15-11 and eventually 19-14. Han's return sailing out on the next point sealed the match and the title for the Indian, who had needed roughly an hour to complete the turnaround.

## A Rare Feat

With the win, Chaliha became only the fifth Indian woman to claim a BWF World Tour title at the Super 300 level or higher, joining Saina Nehwal, P V Sindhu, Devika Sihag and Tanvi Sharma on that list. She is also the first Indian shuttler, male or female, to win the Korea Masters title since the tournament's return to the BWF calendar. The result marks the biggest title of Chaliha's career to date and comes on the back of a run through the draw in which she progressed steadily through the rounds before beating a fellow Indian in the semifinals to reach her first-ever World Tour final.

## Strong Week for Indian Badminton

Chaliha's title arrived on a tournament that also produced a maiden World Tour crown on the men's side, with China's Zhu Xuanchen, ranked 68th in the world, beating South Korea's Yoo Taebin 21-16, 23-21 in the men's singles final. The overall result continues a productive stretch for Indian badminton at the BWF World Tour level, with Indian shuttlers having featured prominently in the latter stages of several Super 300 and Super 500 events through the current season.

For Chaliha specifically, the win is likely to lift her positioning heading into the busier part of the international badminton calendar, giving her a maiden title to build on as she looks to establish herself more consistently among the sport's higher-ranked players. Indian badminton followers will now be watching to see whether the win translates into a deeper run at the next major event on the circuit, with the broader Indian contingent — including established names like Sindhu and Tanvi Sharma — continuing to compete across the World Tour through the rest of the season.
