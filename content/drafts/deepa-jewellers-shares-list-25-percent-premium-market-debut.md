Shares of Deepa Jewellers got off to a strong start on the stock exchanges on Tuesday, listing at a premium of nearly 25 per cent over their issue price and comfortably beating the gains suggested by grey market activity ahead of the debut.

The stock opened at ₹221.05 on the BSE and ₹221 on the NSE, translating into a gain of close to 25 per cent over the initial public offering price of ₹177 per share. That listing pop was notably stronger than the grey market premium seen in the days leading up to the listing, where the stock had been trading at an implied price of around ₹202, or about 14 per cent above the issue price.

## A Stellar Response From Investors

The strong debut followed an equally strong subscription period, during which the issue was booked more than 42 times over. According to exchange data, the company received bids for close to 789 million shares against roughly 18.5 million shares on offer.

Demand was broad-based across investor categories. The non-institutional investor segment led the charge, subscribing nearly 106 times, followed by qualified institutional buyers at around 37 times. The retail portion of the issue, while comparatively more modest, still saw healthy demand at close to 18.5 times subscription.

## About The Company And The Issue

Deepa Jewellers, incorporated in 2016, operates as an organised business-to-business designer, processor and supplier of hallmarked gold jewellery, with its strongest presence across South India. As of the end of July this year, the company counted 373 customers on its books, comprising 47 jewellery retail chains and 326 standalone stores spread across 13 states and one union territory.

The company's total issue size stood at ₹459.72 crore, made up of a fresh issue of ₹250 crore and an offer for sale worth close to ₹209.72 crore from existing shareholders. Of the fresh issue proceeds, the bulk, around ₹215 crore, is earmarked for long-term working capital needs tied to the procurement, maintenance and scaling up of jewellery inventory, with the remainder set aside for general corporate purposes. The price band for the issue had been fixed at ₹168 to ₹177 per share.

## Strong Growth Metrics Ahead Of Listing

Deepa Jewellers has posted rapid growth in the run-up to its stock market debut. Revenue is estimated to have grown at a compound annual rate of roughly 37 per cent between FY2024 and FY2026, while profit after tax rose sharply from ₹24.35 crore to ₹104.79 crore over the same period, a compound annual growth rate of more than 100 per cent.

Following the listing, the company's market capitalisation stood at close to ₹1,970 crore.

## Part Of A Busy IPO Season

Deepa Jewellers' debut comes amid one of the busiest stretches for India's primary market in recent memory, with several companies opening issues in quick succession this month. The strong reception for Deepa Jewellers adds to a string of well-received listings in recent weeks, underscoring healthy retail and institutional appetite for new-age and sector-specific issuers even as the secondary market has turned choppier on account of global crude oil price pressures.
