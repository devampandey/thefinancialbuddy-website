India's tour of Sri Lanka got underway on Saturday morning as the two sides walked out for the first Test of a two-match series at the Galle International Stadium. Shubman Gill, leading India in his new capacity as full-time red-ball captain, won the toss and elected to bat first on a surface that traditionally offers something for both fast bowlers early on and spinners as the match wears on.

## A Fresh Start for Gill's Captaincy

The match marks an early assignment for Gill at the helm of India's Test side, with KL Rahul serving as vice-captain. Galle's pitch has historically rewarded sides that bat first and build a first-innings total before turn becomes pronounced later in the match, and the decision to bat reflects a fairly conventional reading of conditions at the venue. Sri Lanka, led by Dhananjaya de Silva, will have been eyeing early wickets under overcast conditions typical of the coastal ground before having to rely on their spin attack to control the middle overs of India's innings.

## Series Context

The Test forms part of the ongoing World Test Championship cycle, giving both sides an opportunity to pick up early points in the current cycle. It follows India's recent white-ball assignment in Zimbabwe and represents the squad's first red-ball outing since that tour concluded. For Sri Lanka, hosting India at a ground where they have traditionally been competitive gives the home side a chance to test a young side against one of the stronger touring teams in world cricket.

## Early Play

India sent out their top order to begin the innings, with the middle order expected to be tested once the second new ball and spin come into play later in the day. The composition of India's playing eleven reflects a squad in transition, with selectors continuing to manage a broader changeover in the Test set-up following changes to the leadership group earlier this year. Conditions at Galle at the start of play were reported as typical for the ground this time of year, with the pitch expected to assist seam bowlers with the new ball before flattening out for batting through the middle sessions.

## What's at Stake

Beyond the immediate result, the series carries importance for India's positioning in the World Test Championship table and offers an early assessment of how the side functions under its new leadership structure. A strong start with the bat on day one would allow India to set the tone for the rest of the match and put early pressure on a Sri Lankan side playing in familiar home conditions. The second and final Test of the series is scheduled to follow this fixture, giving both teams a chance to respond depending on how this opening match unfolds.

Fans and analysts will be watching closely to see how India's revamped top order settles in in Gill's first assignment as full-time red-ball captain, with the toss decision setting up what is expected to be a closely followed opening day at one of world cricket's most picturesque venues.
