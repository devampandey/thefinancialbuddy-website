India's experimental squad at the SAFF Championship keeps finding ways to win. A first-half penalty from Suhail Ahmad Bhat gave the Blue Tigers a 1-0 win over the Maldives at the National Stadium in Dhaka on Wednesday. The result sealed top spot in Group B with a perfect six points from two games and a place in the semi-finals.

## How the goal came

The decisive moment came in the 38th minute. Abdul Rabeeh won the ball from Maldives defender Hussain Sifaau Yoosuf and was brought down inside the box. Bhat stepped up and sent goalkeeper Hussain Shareef the wrong way, placing his kick into the bottom-left corner. It was his third goal of the tournament after his first-half brace in India's 3-2 win over Pakistan in the opening game, where Manglenthang Kipgen scored the other goal.

## Dubey keeps the lead safe

If Bhat won the game, goalkeeper Priyansh Dubey made sure India kept it. The Maldives pushed hard after the break, and Dubey dealt with a dangerous free-kick in the 79th minute, another effort about five minutes later, and then a point-blank chance from Ethan Ibrahim Zaki in stoppage time. Keeping a clean sheet matters for a young side that conceded twice against Pakistan.

## A deliberately different India team

This is not India's first-choice team. Head coach Khalid Jamil and the senior squad are heading to New Zealand for two friendlies starting November 12, so the SAFF squad is being run by Naushad Moosa, who also coaches the under-23 side. Moosa made seven changes to the starting line-up from the Pakistan game, and Chinglensana Singh Konsham captained the team.

That makes the results more meaningful for India's player pool than the scoreline suggests. Two wins out of two give younger and fringe players competitive minutes in a tournament India has historically dominated. India sit 138th in the FIFA rankings against the Maldives at 173rd, and India have now won four of the last five meetings between the sides, with the Maldives' only win in that run coming in the 2018 final.

## Group table and what comes next

India finished top of Group B on six points, ahead of Pakistan on three, who had already qualified. The Maldives finished without a point and are out.

India's semi-final is on **November 14**. Their opponent will be one of Bangladesh, Bhutan or Sri Lanka, decided by the remaining Group A fixture between hosts Bangladesh and Sri Lanka. A meeting with Bangladesh in front of a home crowd in Dhaka would be the toughest test of this young squad so far.

One frustration for fans: the match was not shown on Indian television and was available only through streaming. Whether broadcasters pick up the knockout games may depend on how far this side goes.
