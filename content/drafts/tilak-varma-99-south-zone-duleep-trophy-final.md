South Zone captain Tilak Varma fell agonisingly short of a century on the fourth morning of the Duleep Trophy 2026 final, dismissed for 99 as his side was bowled out for 336 at the MA Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai, leaving East Zone firmly in control of the title with a 372-run first-innings lead.

## A Captain's Innings Ends in Heartbreak

Resuming the day still needing runs to avoid the follow-on, Varma anchored South Zone's innings with grit, sharing a valuable 78-run partnership for the seventh wicket with Chama Milind before the tail folded around him. He was eventually dismissed attempting to force the pace, chipping a return catch back to bowler Md Kounain Quraishi off a delivery that turned in and looped out of his hands on impact. The dismissal, one run short of what would have been his second successive Duleep Trophy hundred, drew visible sympathy from opposition players, with East Zone's Vaibhav Sooryavanshi and captain Ishan Kishan among those seen applauding Varma as he walked off.

## East Zone's Bowlers Seal a Commanding Lead

Mukesh Kumar led East Zone's bowling effort with three wickets in the morning session, with Mohammed Shami and other seamers sharing the remaining wickets to wrap up South Zone's innings well short of East Zone's mammoth 708. Rather than enforcing the follow-on despite the 372-run cushion, East Zone captain Ishan Kishan opted to send his own batsmen back out to bat again, a decision that reflected the tourists' dominant position and a desire to set South Zone an even more daunting target heading into the final stretch of the match.

East Zone's total of 708 was built around Kishan's own record-breaking 270 — the first double century by an East Zone batter in a Duleep Trophy final — and a supporting 101 from Kumar Kushagra, the pair adding 230 runs together earlier in the match. That platform left South Zone chasing the game from the opening day, with their innings undone by a mix of soft dismissals in the middle order and reverse swing extracted skilfully by Shami and Mukesh Kumar as the ball aged.

## South Zone Face a Battle to Save the Match

With East Zone batting again and openers Abhimanyu Easwaran and Vaibhav Sooryavanshi both dismissed cheaply in the follow-up innings, South Zone's bowlers briefly found rhythm, but the wider picture remains bleak for Tilak Varma's side. Barring a dramatic turnaround, South Zone will need to bat for long periods on the final days simply to avoid defeat, let alone mount a serious challenge for the title.

The result will be a significant marker for both teams heading into the new domestic season, with East Zone's dominant batting and disciplined seam attack likely to strengthen several players' claims for national selection, while South Zone will look to regroup after a final that has, so far, gone almost entirely according to their opponents' script.
