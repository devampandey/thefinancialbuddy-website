Milky Mist Dairy Food, one of India's better-known packaged dairy brands, opened its initial public offering for subscription on Tuesday, giving investors a three-day window to bid for shares in the Rs 1,553 crore issue.

The book-built offer has been priced in a band of Rs 133 to Rs 140 per share, with a lot size of 107 shares. That means retail investors will need to commit roughly Rs 14,980 at the upper end of the price band for a single lot, a fairly accessible entry point compared with several other mainboard issues that have hit the market this year. The subscription window will remain open until August 13, giving investors three full trading days to place their bids before the book closes.

## Structure of the Offer

The IPO combines a fresh issue of shares aggregating up to roughly Rs 1,428 crore with an offer for sale of close to Rs 125 crore worth of shares from existing stakeholders. The bulk of the capital being raised, in other words, will flow directly into the company rather than simply allowing early investors to cash out, which is often viewed favourably by analysts assessing the growth intent behind an issue. Proceeds from the fresh issue are expected to go toward debt reduction, capacity expansion and general corporate purposes, in line with what dairy and packaged foods companies have typically prioritised when tapping public markets in recent years.

## About the Company

Incorporated in 2014, Milky Mist has built a fairly diversified product portfolio that spans cheese, paneer, butter, curd, ghee, yoghurt, ice cream, UHT milk products, frozen foods, and ready-to-eat and ready-to-cook items, along with a smaller chocolates business. That breadth sets it apart from some peers that remain concentrated in one or two dairy categories, and gives the company multiple growth levers as Indian consumers continue shifting toward branded, packaged dairy over loose, unbranded alternatives.

## Timeline Ahead

Once the subscription window closes on August 13, the company is expected to finalise its basis of allotment around August 14, followed by refunds and the credit of shares to successful applicants around August 17. Shares are tentatively scheduled to make their stock exchange debut on the NSE on August 18, a listing timeline that mirrors the standard T+3 process most Indian IPOs now follow after recent regulatory streamlining.

## What Investors Are Watching

The IPO arrives at a busy moment for India's primary market, with several other mainboard and SME issues either opening or closing around the same window this week. As with most consumer and FMCG-adjacent listings, investor interest is likely to hinge on subscription trends in the retail and institutional categories over the next three days, as well as unofficial grey market cues, though such indicators tend to be volatile and are not always a reliable predictor of listing-day performance. Analysts typically point to Milky Mist's brand recognition, distribution reach and category diversification as the key factors likely to influence how the issue is received once bidding gets underway in earnest.
