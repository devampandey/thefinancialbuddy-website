Indian equity benchmarks started Tuesday's session on a cautious note, extending a hesitant tone that has marked trading over the past several sessions. The BSE Sensex opened down around 109 to 177 points, or roughly 0.14 to 0.23 per cent, slipping toward the 77,190-77,260 range, while the NSE Nifty 50 shed about 80 to 83 points, or close to 0.33 per cent, to trade near 24,138 in the opening hour.

## What Set The Tone

The soft start followed signals from Gift Nifty, which was trading about 0.13 per cent lower ahead of the domestic open, pointing to a range-bound session as traders looked for fresh cues. With no major domestic catalysts on the calendar, investors largely stayed on the sidelines, digesting the past week's moves in crude oil, US bond yields and global risk sentiment rather than reacting to any single dominant headline.

## Sector And Stock Moves

The session was not uniformly weak. On the Sensex, Trent and Eternal led gainers, followed by TCS, Tata Steel and Adani Ports, while Maruti Suzuki, Power Grid, HCL Technologies and Tech Mahindra were among the laggards dragging on the index. Sectorally, the Nifty Media index bucked the broader trend with a gain of close to 1 per cent, and Nifty PSU Bank also edged higher by about 0.3 per cent, suggesting pockets of buying interest even as the headline indices stayed in the red.

Stock-specific action added to the morning's volume, with Hindustan Copper shares swinging sharply lower after the government opened an offer for sale in the company at a steep discount to Monday's closing price, while a clutch of other names remained in focus around ongoing IPO listings, quarterly earnings catch-up disclosures and routine corporate actions.

## The Bigger Picture

Tuesday's muted start comes against the backdrop of a market that has struggled to find a sustained direction in recent sessions, with indices oscillating between modest gains and losses as investors weigh domestic growth signals against global cross-currents including elevated crude oil prices and the trajectory of US interest rates. Traders will be watching whether the afternoon session brings any pickup in volumes or a clearer directional bias, particularly as more companies work through the tail end of the June-quarter earnings cycle and as global markets continue to digest recent commentary out of Washington on trade and monetary policy.

For now, the tone remains one of consolidation rather than conviction, with both benchmarks holding within a relatively narrow band of their recent trading range as the session progresses.
