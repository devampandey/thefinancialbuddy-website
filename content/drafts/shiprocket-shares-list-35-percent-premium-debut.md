Shiprocket, the e-commerce logistics and shipping platform, made a stellar debut on Indian stock exchanges on Wednesday, with its shares listing at a roughly 35% premium over its issue price, handing early investors a strong first-day gain.

## Listing Details

Shares of Shiprocket listed at ₹131 on the NSE, a premium of about 35% over the IPO issue price of ₹97, while the stock opened at a similar level on the BSE. The strong opening reflected robust investor appetite that had been building through the company's book-built public offering, which raised a total of ₹1,617 crore.

The issue comprised a combination of a fresh share issuance and an offer for sale by existing shareholders, and was open for subscription between August 12 and August 14. By the close of bidding, the offering had been subscribed close to 99 times overall, with the qualified institutional buyer category, non-institutional investors and retail investors all participating heavily, translating into bids worth well over ₹90,000 crore against the issue size. Allotment was finalized on August 17, ahead of Wednesday's listing.

## About the Company

Shiprocket operates a logistics and shipping technology platform that helps e-commerce sellers, particularly small and medium-sized businesses, manage order fulfillment, shipping and delivery logistics across couriers and marketplaces. The company has built its business around simplifying the often-fragmented logistics process for online sellers in India, a market that has grown rapidly alongside the broader expansion of e-commerce and direct-to-consumer brands in the country.

The IPO proceeds from the fresh issue portion are expected to support the company's continued investment in its logistics technology and operations, while the offer-for-sale component allowed some existing investors to partially monetize their holdings as part of the listing.

## Part of a Busier IPO Window

Shiprocket's listing came on a day that also saw Behari Lal Engineering, an integrated metal solutions company, begin trading after raising ₹302 crore through its own public issue, underscoring a relatively active primary market this week even as broader secondary market sentiment stayed cautious amid global headwinds. Shiprocket's debut followed a string of other recent listings on Indian exchanges that have drawn strong retail and institutional interest, reflecting continued appetite for new-economy and logistics-focused businesses among Indian investors.

## What It Means for Investors

A 35% listing-day gain places Shiprocket among the stronger debuts seen on Indian exchanges this year, and will likely be closely watched by investors weighing participation in the pipeline of upcoming public offerings from other consumer internet and logistics companies. Analysts note that while strong listing-day pops can reflect genuine investor enthusiasm for a company's growth prospects, they can also be influenced by broader liquidity conditions and sentiment in the primary market at the time of listing, meaning sustained post-listing performance will be the more meaningful test of the stock going forward.
